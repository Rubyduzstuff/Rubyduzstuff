### 🛠️ What I do

I work in IT and use software development to remove the repetitive, manual parts of the job, for my team and for the people we support.

- **🚀 Remote software deployment:** packaging and rolling out software to Windows and Mac fleets with Intune, SCCM and Jamf, without anyone leaving their desk.
- **⚙️ Bespoke software configuration:** tailoring applications, policies and settings to how each team actually works.
- **📊 Inventory data analytics:** turning hardware and software inventory data into answers with SQL, Python and Power BI: what's out there, what's out of date, and what needs attention.
- **📜 Deployment scripts:** repeatable, reliable PowerShell and Python scripts that replace click-by-click setup.
- **🤝 Internal tools:** small utilities that take a tedious task off a colleague's plate.
