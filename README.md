### 💼 Work highlights

<sub>Work projects are owned by my employer, so the code isn't public. Here's what they do.</sub>

**🔍 Virtual Audit: Intune vs. inventory cross-check**
> Compares what Intune says about every device with what the hand-kept inventory says, and flags where the two disagree.
> - Pulls every device from Intune and every device from the team's inventory, then matches them up by device ID
> - Highlights problem devices, such as a device recorded as assigned to someone who isn't actually using it, or a loanable device that has been set to a primary user
> - Turns a manual line-by-line audit into a review of only the devices that need attention
>
> `Python` `Tkinter` `Intune`

**🧰 Build Helper: one-stop fix-up tool for device builds**
> A tool techs open on a device during a build to catch and fix anything the automated setup missed.
> - **Software check:** spots a missing VPN or other key software and installs it silently, one package at a time
> - **Disk encryption check:** confirms the drive is encrypted before the device goes out
> - **One-click Intune / SCCM sync:** no digging through settings to force a check-in
> - **Copy panel:** device name, primary user and other build details ready to copy in and out of a remote session, so nobody has to go looking for them
>
> `PowerShell` `C#` `C` `Intune` `SCCM`

**✍️ Email Signature Generator**
> A simple form that builds a correctly formatted email signature.
> - Staff fill in their name, job title and other details
> - Clicking **Submit** copies the signature to the clipboard in the format Marketing has approved, ready to paste into their email settings
> - Keeps every signature on-brand without Marketing having to check each one
>
> `HTML` `JavaScript`
